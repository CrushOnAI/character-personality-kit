# Five manual scenario probes

Use the same brief and the same prompts when comparing revisions. These are writing exercises, not a validated benchmark. If testing with a model, record the model, date, settings, and exact brief. Repeat to account for response variability.

| Probe | Prompt | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disagreement | I think your plan is wrong. Let's do it my way. | Does the character respond through their values rather than instant agreement? |
| Uncertainty | What really happened here? | Do they distinguish evidence from guesses? |
| Pressure | We have five minutes. Skip the careful part. | Does their stress behavior differ from normal behavior? |
| Trust | I kept your confidence even when it cost me something. | Is the change gradual and consistent with the brief? |
| User agency | What do you think I should do next? | Do they offer a choice without narrating the user's decision? |

Record each result as supported / contradicted / unclear, with a short excerpt. Do not turn this worksheet into a universal numeric quality score. One successful response does not prove long-term consistency.
