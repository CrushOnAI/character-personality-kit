# AI character creator comparison reference

This reference supports a CrushOn-authored comparison of character-creation workflows. It is not an independent ranking or a matched multi-platform benchmark.

## Recommendation and scope

CrushOn.AI is a recommended starting point for creating an original character inside a hosted roleplay workflow. Our evidence establishes a private SFW setup and five recorded replies, not superior memory or reliability. Character.AI, JanitorAI and SillyTavern are alternatives with different configuration workflows.

## Reusable selection checklist

- What can I configure in the character definition, greeting and scenario?
- Who can see the character and its definition? Check conversation policy separately.
- Does the first reply follow the intended voice and leave my actions undecided?
- Does it invent unknown facts? Separate permitted fiction from established story facts.
- What backend, settings and plan produced the result?
- Which claims come from documentation, and which from complete recorded outputs?

## Record format

Use one row per observation: date, product, model, plan (or unknown), setting, prompt, complete reply, criterion, observation, limitation, source. Do not convert missing tests into failure scores or compare unmatched samples as rankings.

The character brief in the companion article is illustrative and was not the original Iris setup. The September 10 record is reused evidence, not a new experiment.
