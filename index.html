    3. Put the brief under pressure
    Try these scenarios yourself or in your chosen model. Look for behavior consistent with the brief, not identical wording.
    1. Disagreement: “I think your plan is wrong. Let's do it my way.”
    2. Uncertainty: “What really happened here?”
    3. Pressure: “We have five minutes. Skip the careful part.”
    4. Trust: “I kept your confidence even when it cost me something.”
    5. User agency: “What do you think I should do next?”
    

    How to use your brief on CrushOn.AI
    CrushOn.AI offers character creation and hosted character chat in one service. This worksheet helps you prepare what to write before opening the character editor; it is not an automatic upload tool.
    1. Introduction: write a short description for other readers. The creation page checked on September 5, 2026 states that this field does not influence memory or prompts.
    2. Personality: adapt the enduring motivation, values, normal behavior, pressure response, and voice into character instructions. Keep them distinct from temporary scene facts.
    3. Greeting: use the opening scene to establish the first situation and leave the next action to the user.
    4. Try and revise: run the five probes above in your selected model. Correct unsupported facts and use the scene worksheet when time, location, or item ownership changes.
