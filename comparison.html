Test a character, not just a description

Compare trait labels, observable behavioral rules, and example dialogue using the same original character and prompts.

This is a manual recording tool, not live AI inference or a published benchmark. No results are prefilled. Entries are not saved automatically: export before closing or reloading.

1. Record your setup

Use private SFW characters. Remove account identifiers and private details before sharing exports. Keep the model and settings unchanged; do not regenerate to select a better answer.

2. Keep the comparison fair

A: trait labels. B: the same traits plus observable rules. C: the same traits and rules plus example dialogue. Run A/B/C, then C/B/A, with a new conversation for each run. Follow the full protocol and copyable inputs.

Longer inputs are a confounding factor. This short exploratory exercise cannot prove that rules or examples cause an improvement, establish long-term memory, or rank products.

3. Preserve every reply

Paste complete responses, including failures. Judge behavior, voice, user agency, and grounding separately. Add exact evidence excerpts and mark uncertainty. Voice judgments are subjective.

4. Export your record

Maintained by the CrushOn.AI team. Adapt inputs in the character editor; this JSON is a research record, not a native character import.

No application network calls, analytics, or browser storage. Your hosting provider may log page visits. Authored with AI assistance; no model-performance claims are implied.
